The leak

A gym doesn't lose members. It forgets them.

Priya's plan ended on the 3rd. She meant to renew. Nobody messaged her. By the 20th she'd joined the studio down the road. You never even knew she'd left — until the month's collection came up short.

That's silent churn: not a complaint, not a cancellation, just a quiet gap in the roster that nobody was watching.

  • The diary Expiry dates live in a register at the desk. Nobody reads yesterday's page, so nobody knows who lapsed.
  • WhatsApp by memory You remind the members you happen to remember. The quiet ones — the ones already drifting — get nothing.
  • Trials that vanish A trial walks in, sweats it out, walks off. No follow-up, no conversion, no record it ever happened.
  • Freezes gone wrong A member pauses for a wedding trip. You forget to extend the end date. Now it's an argument at the desk.

The wedge

Gyms don't die from bad workouts. They die from members lapsing without a single reminder. Fix the reminder, and you keep the member.

So GymKhata does exactly one job well: the renewal, win-back, and trial-conversion engine — nothing else.

Do my members need to install an app?

No. GymKhata runs on your phone or the reception tablet. Members just get the reminders you send — over the channels they already use.

How does the win-back ladder actually reach people?

GymKhata writes the message for each member and drops it into one outbox — expiring, expired, and 30-day lapsed. You review and tap send. You stay in control of every message that goes out.

What happens when a member freezes their plan?

You enter the freeze dates and GymKhata pushes the end date out by exactly those days. The membership stays fair, and the record is right the next time they walk in.

Pricing is worked out on a quick discovery call and billed monthly on subscription. We're keeping public prices off the page while we onboard our first users.
